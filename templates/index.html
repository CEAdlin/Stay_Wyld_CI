About The Wyld Collective

New for 2026, The Wyld Collective is becoming a beautifully designed, nature-centric retreat where slow living, woodland calm and handcrafted luxury come together.

At the heart of The Wyld Collective are our five bespoke glamping retreats — each one hand-built on site by us. You’ll find a characterful mix of extra-large shepherds huts, rustic solid-timber A-frames and beautifully finished woodland lodges, all created with care, craft and a deep respect for the land around us.

We’re lucky enough to be nestled within an established, magical woodland, and we embrace it fully. Fairy-lights wind through the trees, hidden seating nooks invite quiet moments, and winding woodland paths lead you to a whole menagerie of friendly animals.

Come and meet our three gentle Shetland ponies, our rare-breed chickens, and the ever-expanding guinea-pig village that delights guests of all ages.

Over the coming months, our coffee hut and pizza shack will transform into an exotic greenhouse café set inside the old commercial glasshouse. Natural hessian drapes, warm fairy lights and lush foliage will create a calm, overgrown sanctuary. Resin pathways will lead to private seating areas with cosy sofas and comfortable chairs, all centred around a stone-edged pond home to our koi. In cooler months, wood-burning stoves will keep the space snug, while outside, covered pergolas sit among silver birches, olive trees and magnolias for those who prefer to relax beneath the open sky.

We’re also building a children’s play barn, complete with small-world farm play where little ones can ride around the mini farm track on their own tractors, collect eggs and milk from the farm stands, and head off on their delivery rounds. For those who prefer mud pies to milk runs, our woodland mud kitchen is ready and waiting.

As we grow, we’re bringing the poly-tunnels and orchard back to life using permaculture principles and organic growing methods. Over the coming months and years, The Wyld Collective will continue to evolve into a little slice of paradise — a place shaped gently by nature, crafted by hand, and shared with those who want to slow down and reconnect.

We’d love to welcome you as we create something truly special here on the edge of the Staffordshire countryside.
